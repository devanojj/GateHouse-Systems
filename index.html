Services

Everything your systems need, under one roof

Whether you need an extra pair of hands or someone to own IT end to end, we cover the day-to-day and the projects.

Microsoft 365 administration

Tenant setup and migration, Exchange Online, SharePoint and Teams, licensing reviews, security baselines, MFA and conditional access.

Azure & cloud support

Security & ongoing maintenance

About

A straightforward IT partner

GateHouse Systems is an IT support and systems administration practice working with small and growing organisations. We handle the Microsoft 365 and Azure estate, the laptops and phones, the network in the wall, and the person who cannot get into their mailbox on a Monday morning.

The approach is deliberately unglamorous: understand what you actually run, fix what is broken, document it, and keep it steady. No jargon, no drawn-out projects, no surprises on the invoice.
